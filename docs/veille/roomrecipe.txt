Need to know 
Frequently asked questions. 
Everything you need to know before finding products and trying them in your space. 
 Still have a question? Contact us → 
 How does RoomRecipe find products? + 
RoomRecipe analyzes the furniture and décor visible in a room image and looks for matching products from online retailers. Results help you explore the style, materials and pieces that make up the room. 
 Can I use any inspiration photo? + 
You can use a room photo, a screenshot, or inspiration you&#x27;ve saved. JPEG, PNG and WebP are supported. Clear, well-lit photos with visible furniture give the best results. 
 Can I see products in my own room? + 
Yes. Select the products you want to try and add a photo of your own room to create a visual preview. Use it to explore the look; check product measurements before ordering. 
 Are the products connected to real stores? + 
Product results in RoomRecipe include links to online retailers so you can review current pricing, availability and details directly with the store. 
 Can I save the rooms and products I like? + 
Keep your inspiration and product choices together in RoomRecipe. In this interactive example, the heart buttons save your favorite pieces in this browser. 
 Is RoomRecipe available on iOS and Android? + 
Yes. RoomRecipe is available on the App Store and Google Play. You can also get started in your web browser. 
 What counts as one analysis? + 
One room photo = one analysis. RoomRecipe identifies the pieces in it and returns a product list with prices and store links. 
 What happens after my free analyses? + 
After your 2 free analyses you can move to a monthly plan from $2.99/month. Extra visualization credits start at $0.79. No credit card is needed to start. 
 How long does an analysis take? + 
Most analyses are ready in under 2 minutes. Busy times or very detailed rooms can take a little longer. 
 What happens to my photos? + 
Your photos are stored securely in your RoomRecipe account so you can come back to your rooms and results. They are never shared with or sold to third parties.
