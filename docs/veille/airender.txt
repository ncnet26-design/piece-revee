 FAQ 
Common questions 
 What is airender? 
airender is an AI room redesign and shopping app for homeowners and renters. You snap a photo of a room, pick a style like Scandinavian or Japandi, and AI redesigns the space in about 60 seconds, then gives you a real shopping list of the exact furniture and decor, with prices and buy links from stores that ship to your country. It&#x27;s made for everyday people redecorating a room, not for professional 3D, CAD, or architectural workflows. 
 Are these real products I can actually buy? 
Yes. The shopping list is built from real product listings at real retailers, matched to your design. Each item shows the merchant, the price, and a working buy link. It&#x27;s a buyable plan, not a moodboard. 
 Will the products ship to my country? 
We localize the whole list to your region. You see prices in your currency from stores that actually deliver to you. Live in the US, UK, or core EU today, with 70+ countries supported. A shopper in Germany sees EUR and EU stores; a US shopper sees USD and US stores. 
 Is the design exactly what&#x27;s in the picture? 
It&#x27;s a close depiction. The listed product is the real, buyable one, but AI renders aren&#x27;t pixel-perfect SKUs, so think of it as &#x27;shop this look.&#x27; You get the cohesive room plus the real items that recreate it. 
 Do I need any design skills? 
None. Upload a photo of your room, pick a style like Scandinavian or Japandi, and the AI does the rest. No measuring, no mood-boarding, no designer fees. 
 Does it really replace my furniture, or just add to it? 
It&#x27;s a full gut-and-restyle, not clutter piled on top of what&#x27;s there. The AI reimagines the room from the ground up in your chosen style, then matches real products to the result. 
 Is it free to try? 
Yes. You get 2 full room redesigns free, including the full shopping list with prices and buy links, no credit card needed. You only pay if you want unlimited restyles, edits and more rooms, from $19. 
 Can I change a single piece or the whole budget? 
Yes. Swap any item for a cheaper or pricier alternate and the running total updates instantly. You can also describe a change in plain words, like &#x27;make the sofa darker&#x27; or &#x27;more plants,&#x27; and re-render just that. 
 What kinds of rooms work? 
Living rooms, bedrooms, studios, kitchens, even empty rooms you just moved into. A brighter, straight-on photo gives the best result, but cluttered or dated rooms work fine too. 
 Is airender.ai the same as AiRender Studio? 
No. airender.ai is a room redesign and shopping app for homeowners and renters. AiRender Studio, airender.pro, airender.io and airender.com are separate products by other companies for architects rendering 3D models in SketchUp, Revit or Blender. airender.ai has no connection to them and does not render architectural models. 
Not sure what you want yet? 
Start with a style you like or a budget you have. Every guide lists real products with prices checked against the merchant, and you can drop any of them into your own room.
