 What is AI room redesign? + 
AI room redesign uses computer vision to transform photos of your existing rooms into photorealistic renderings in different interior design styles. Upload a photo, pick a style like Modern, Scandinavian, or Japandi, and EasyRoomAI generates a styled version of your actual space in seconds. How much does it cost to use EasyRoomAI? + 
You get one free watermarked redesign every 24 hours without signing up, plus 5 starter credits when you create a free account. One-time credit packs start at $1.99, and monthly plans start at $19.90/month with 1,000 credits, HD downloads, and commercial use rights. What design styles are available? + 
EasyRoomAI includes 16 curated interior design styles: Modern, Minimalist, Scandinavian, Industrial, Bohemian, Japandi, Mid-Century Modern, Coastal, Farmhouse, Art Deco, Traditional, Contemporary, Mediterranean, Tropical, Rustic, and Wabi-Sabi. All styles are included in every plan. Can I use AI-generated designs for real estate listings? + 
Yes. Real estate agents and property photographers use EasyRoomAI for 2D and 360° virtual staging on Zillow, Realtor.com, MLS, and Airbnb. Paid plans and credit packs include full commercial use rights. How is this different from traditional interior design software? + 
Traditional tools like SketchUp or 3ds Max require manual 3D modeling and hours of rendering. EasyRoomAI works directly from a single photo: no floor plans, no modeling, and no learning curve. Upload a photo, pick a style, and get results in under 30 seconds. Is my data private and secure? + 
Yes. Your uploaded photos and generated designs are private by default. We do not sell your images to third parties or use private client photos to train public models. You retain full ownership of your generated designs.
