Common questions Is the free tier really free? + 
Yes. Two full redesigns, no credit card required. Just create an account and upload your first photo. What is the Room Plan? + 
A one-time $19 upgrade for one room. The shopping list is already free; the Room Plan adds unlimited restyles and edits on that room, swapping any piece for another, and a room plan you can save as a PDF. It never renews. If you are furnishing several rooms, Whole Home covers every room for $75. What counts as one redesign? + 
One photo in, one redesigned room out, with a shopping list. Requesting multiple styles of the same room each count as one redesign. What is in the shopping list? + 
Every item visible in the render, with real prices and buy links, a running budget total, and alternatives for every item. It is free on every plan, and you can add the whole room to your Amazon cart in one tap. Do you offer refunds? + 
No — and that&#x27;s why the free tier exists. You get two full redesigns before spending anything, so you can see exactly what you&#x27;re buying. Because you can try the real product first, paid upgrades are final. If you were charged in error or double-billed, email info@airender.ai and we&#x27;ll fix it. Does Whole Home include future updates? + 
Yes. One payment gets you every new style, feature, and improvement we ship, forever.
