The difference: GENA does the maths 
Image generators put down a sofa wider than the wall behind it. GENA measured that wall first and checks every product it finds against that number — before you click buy. 
Precise measuring — walls, windows and doors in centimetres — is available from the Hobby plan; the more exact the measurement, the more reliable the fit badge on every product in the shopping list.

Frequently asked questions 
 What is GENA Interior? 
GENA Interior is an AI room planner: you upload a photo of your room, GENA measures walls, windows and doors in centimetres and then refurnishes the room photorealistically — with real furniture that provably fits, including price and buy link. Built for renters, homeowners and anyone who wants to see a room before furnishing or selling it. How accurate are the measurements from the photo? 
GENA derives the measurements from the photo — good enough for proportions and furniture suggestions. From a single image the error is typically around ten percent. Before you buy, it is best to measure yourself. Is GENA suitable for virtual home staging? 
Yes. Because GENA measures the real room and checks every piece of furniture against that measurement, you get precise virtual staging instead of image-generator décor — ideal for showing a room before furnishing or selling. What does GENA Interior cost? 
GENA starts free with 5 generations per month, no credit card. Hobby (€14.99 a month or €149 a year, equal to €12.42/month) gives you 50 generations and 20 room analyses per month; Pro (€24.99 a month or €249 a year, equal to €20.75/month) gives you 300 generations, unlimited analyses, 4K export, API access and commercial use — monthly plans cancellable monthly, yearly plans at the end of the term, no minimum term beyond that. Can I cancel anytime? 
Yes. On the monthly plan, Hobby and Pro are cancellable monthly, there is no minimum term, and you cancel directly in the app. The yearly plan (€149 or €249 a year, paid upfront) can be cancelled any time to the end of the term. Free needs no subscription and stays at 5 generations per month. Is the result labelled as an AI image? 
Yes. Every result is labelled as AI-generated — visibly in the image and machine-readable in the metadata — as required by Article 50 of the EU AI Act, including on Pro and in 4K.
