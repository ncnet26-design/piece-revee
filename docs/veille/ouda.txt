What is Ouda? 
Ouda is an interior design tool that turns one photo of your room into a photorealistic render of that same room, furnished. 
Take one photo of the room you have now. Any room, any light, any mess. Ouda sends it back furnished, using the same space at the same proportions, so what you get is your room and not a showroom. It works from the room as it stands, so there is nothing to clear, stage or measure first. Every piece in the render comes from a partner retailer's catalogue. Tap one and you get the vendor and the price, so you can order it. That is the difference between a mood board and a room you can buy.

Common questions. 
One photo is a good place to start. Here's what else to know. 
 What is Ouda? 
Ouda is an interior design tool that turns one photo of your room into a photorealistic render of that same room, furnished. Every object in the render is a real product from a partner retailer. Tap one and you get the shop selling it and the price. 
 How does Ouda work? 
Three steps. You photograph the room you already have. Ouda redesigns it and sends back a photorealistic picture of the same space. Then you tap any piece in that picture to see which retailer sells it and what it costs. 
 Are the products in an Ouda render real? 
Yes. Renders are built from the catalogues of partner retailers, not from furniture the model invented. Nothing in the picture is a piece you then have to go hunting for. Tap any object and you get the shop, the price and a way to buy it. 
 Which retailers does Ouda source from? 
Ten retail partners so far: Electro Sakr, Societe Sakr, Wood And Gas, Solet Tapis, Panda Boutique, Amazona Paints, Colortek, Matta Gallery, Al Moustawda3 and Abed Tahan. The list keeps growing. 
 Do I need a special camera, angle or floor plan? 
No. One photo does it. Any room, any light, any mess. There is no angle you need to get right and no floor plan to draw. Ouda works from the room exactly as it is right now. 
 How can I try Ouda? 
Ouda is available now. Open the app, photograph your room and get it back furnished with real products you can buy.
